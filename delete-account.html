BuyVote Account Deletion

BuyVote allows users to delete their account and associated data.

Delete your account in the BuyVote app

  1. Open BuyVote and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps to permanently delete your account.

If you cannot access the app

You can request account deletion by contacting us at YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL. Please contact us using the email address associated with your BuyVote account so that we can verify the request.

Data that will be deleted

When your account is deleted, BuyVote will delete data associated with your account, including:

  • Your BuyVote account and profile information
  • Friend connections associated with your account
  • Purchase requests associated with your account
  • Uploaded purchase images associated with your account
  • Approval and rejection information associated with your account
  • Notification and device token information associated with your account

Data retention

Account data is deleted when the account deletion process is completed. Certain information may be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.
