Privacy Policy for BuyVote

Effective date: September 21, 2026

BuyVote helps users submit purchase requests to trusted friends for approval or rejection before making a purchase.

Information We Collect

BuyVote may collect and process the following information:

  • Account information, such as email address and profile information.
  • Friend and connection information used to manage approval relationships.
  • Purchase request information, including product name, price, reason, and selected approver.
  • Images uploaded by users as part of purchase requests.
  • Approval or rejection decisions and optional approval notes.
  • Notification device tokens used to deliver push notifications.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Allow users to connect with friends.
  • Send and review purchase approval requests.
  • Store approval history.
  • Send app notifications.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Service Providers

BuyVote uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the service. These may include Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend services, and Google Firebase for push notification services.

Data Sharing

Purchase request information is intended to be visible only to the user who created the request and the friend selected to review it, subject to the application's access controls.

We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information, including authenticated access and access controls for private application data.

Data Retention and Deletion

User information is retained while an account remains active or as necessary to provide the service. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data through the account deletion feature in the BuyVote app.

Additional account deletion instructions may also be provided through our public account deletion page.

Camera and Photo Library

BuyVote may request access to the camera or photo library when a user chooses to attach an image to a purchase request. This access is used only when the user initiates the relevant feature.

Children

BuyVote is not specifically designed for children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
